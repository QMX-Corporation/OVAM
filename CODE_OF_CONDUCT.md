# QMX Corporation Community Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Pledge
We, as maintainers and contributors of the OVAM (Open Vallen Ard Man) project, pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, or personal beliefs.

We are here to write pure, high-performance low-level code, eliminate legacy bloatware, and respect the hardware.

## 2. Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other community members.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and architectural approaches.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on Pull Requests.
- Focusing on what is best for the silicon, the performance, and the security of the Ring 0 architecture.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment of any member.
- Submitting legacy code, spaghetti code, or unsafe pointers without proper documentation.

## 3. Enforcement Responsibilities
Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, fair, and corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

**The Project Administrator (dev12124) has the final authority to reject Pull Requests or ban members who violate these rules.**

## 4. Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including issues, Pull Requests, discussion forums, and direct messages associated with the QMX Corporation ecosystem.
